Simple Interest Method & Terms

• Calculation Method: Interest is calculated linearly without compounding over time: Time in years = total months / 12, Simple Interest = Principal × (Annual Rate / 100) × Time in years, and Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest.
• Zero Rate Behavior: If the annual interest rate is 0%, total interest is strictly ₹0 and the total amount equals the principal amount.
• No Reinvestment: Unlike compound interest or growth funds, earnings are not added back into the principal for subsequent periods. Taxes, deductions, or penalties are not deducted.

Compound Interest Formula & Compounding Frequencies

• Standard Mathematical Formula: A = P × (1 + r/n)n×t, where P = Principal, r = Annual Rate / 100, n = Compounding periods per year (Yearly = 1, Half-Yearly = 2, Quarterly = 4), and t = Time in years (total months / 12).
• Zero Rate Behavior: When the annual interest rate is 0%, interest earned is strictly ₹0 and total estimated value equals the principal amount.
• Planning Estimates: Returns are mathematical projections assuming constant rates and periodic compounding without intermediate withdrawals, tax deductions, or inflation adjustments.

Income Tax Rules & Documentation Requirements

• Salary Definition (Rule 2A): Salary for HRA exemption calculation includes Basic Salary plus Dearness Allowance (DA) if it forms part of retirement benefits. Other allowances and bonuses are excluded.
• Section 10(13A) Exemption: Under the Old Tax Regime, the exempt HRA is strictly the minimum of: (1) Actual HRA received, (2) Rent paid minus 10% of salary, or (3) 50% of salary for metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) and 40% for non-metro cities.
• Proof & Landlord PAN: Under Section 206AA / ITD guidelines, if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000 (₹8,333/month), your landlord's PAN must be reported to your employer along with valid rent receipts and rental agreement.
• Disclaimer: This calculator provides estimated figures for financial planning. Actual tax liability depends on your total income, deductions, and tax slabs. Consult a certified tax advisor or CA.

Statutory Lock-in Period & Legal Regulations

• Mandatory 3-Year Lock-in: Under Equity Linked Savings Scheme Guidelines, 2005 and Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, ELSS investments are subject to a compulsory 3-year (36 months) lock-in from the unit allotment date. No premature withdrawals or redemption requests can be processed before this period.
• SIP Rolling Lock-in: When investing through monthly Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), each individual installment carries its own independent 3-year lock-in period from its transaction date.
• Tax Law Framework: Deductions under Section 80C are limited to ₹1,50,000 per financial year under the Old Tax Regime. Under Section 115BAC (New Tax Regime), Section 80C deductions are not available. Capital gains on redemption are subject to Section 112A (LTCG exceeding ₹1.25 lakh per financial year is taxed at 12.5% plus applicable surcharge/cess under Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024).
• Market-Linked Disclaimer: ELSS mutual funds invest primarily in equity shares and are subject to market risks.
